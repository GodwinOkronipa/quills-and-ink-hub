QUILLS AND INK HUB — MEMORIAL BROCHURE CHECKLIST
============================================================
Essential components for a Ghanaian memorial service program / brochure.

1. FRONT COVER & FRONTISPIECE
[ ] High-resolution portrait photograph of loved one
[ ] Full legal name and affectionately known names / clan titles
[ ] Sunrise and Sunset dates (Birth and Passing)
[ ] Service venue, date, and officiating ministers/clergy
[ ] Theme scripture, hymn title, or family quote

2. ORDER OF SERVICE
[ ] Musical prelude & processional hymn
[ ] Opening prayer and purpose of gathering
[ ] Scripture readings (Old & New Testament)
[ ] Biography / Life sketch presentation
[ ] Tributes section (Widow/Widower, Children, Siblings, Grandchildren, In-laws, Church/Organization)
[ ] Sermon / Homily / Exhortation
[ ] Offering & announcements
[ ] Commendation & recessional hymn

3. FAMILY TRIBUTES & PHOTOS
[ ] Children's collective or individual tributes
[ ] Extended family & clan acknowledgment (Abusua)
[ ] Photo gallery: Youth, marriage, career, family travels, milestones
[ ] Condolences & appreciation message from family

4. RECEPTION & APPRECIATION
[ ] Reception venue directions and seating order
[ ] Acknowledgment of well-wishers and diaspora contributors

QUILLS AND INK HUB — EULOGY WRITING & REFLECTION GUIDE
============================================================
A gentle roadmap for families crafting a memorial tribute or eulogy.

1. THE FOUNDATION: WHO WERE THEY TO THOSE THEY LOVED?
- What name or title brought warmth to their face?
- What was their favorite saying, phrase, or gentle habit?
- What values guided their decisions (faith, family, education, discipline, hospitality)?

2. STRUCTURE OF A DIGNIFIED EULOGY
- Introduction: Express gratitude to attendees and set a comforting, reverent tone.
- Early Life & Lineage: Acknowledge roots, birthplace, ancestry, and formative moments.
- Life's Work & Passion: Career, community service, church fellowship, or family building.
- Personal Anecdotes: 1-2 vivid, specific memories that illustrate their character.
- Reflections & Legacy: What teachings and values live on in children and grandchildren?
- Farewell & Benediction: A closing blessing or words of peace.

3. ADVICE FOR SPOKEN DELIVERY
- Aim for 5 to 7 minutes (approximately 650 to 900 words).
- Speak slowly and pause when emotion arises; the congregation is standing with you.
- Designate a family member or our literary coordinator as a gentle backup reader.
